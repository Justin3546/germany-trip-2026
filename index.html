A car drove us from Europa-Park to Grindelwald in the Swiss Alps this morning, and we finally got our first look at the snowy peaks. In the afternoon we took the cable car up to Pfingstegg and did the mountain toboggan and the Fly-Line zipline at the top. The kids also found a rope swing and a playground zip line. We finished the afternoon in bathrobes back at the hotel.

Jungfraujoch✓ Recap

Wednesday, October 14
Jungfraujoch
8:45 AM
Eiger Express gondola Grindelwald Terminal to Eigergletscher
Morning
Jungfrau Railway Eigergletscher to Jungfraujoch, with a stop at Eismeer
Morning
Snow and the Ice Palace Jungfraujoch, Top of Europe
Afternoon
Indoor swimming Sportzentrum Grindelwald

Great day up at the Jungfraujoch, the Top of Europe. We took the Eiger Express gondola up from Grindelwald, then the train through the mountain with a quick stop at the Eismeer windows, and the kids played in the snow at the top making snowballs and snow angels. We also walked through the Ice Palace and got a new cap for Cora.

In the afternoon we swam in the indoor pool at the Sportzentrum in the village, then had room service for dinner at the hotel.

Big day up at First. We took the gondola up and did the Cliff Walk, but skipped the line out to the very edge because it was really long. Then we hiked about a mile toward Bachalpsee before the kids got tired and we turned back, had lunch at the top, and did the First Flyer (Ricky's pick, which looks scary but was a lot of fun) and the First Glider, which was awesome.

Back at the hotel we rested, then hit the pool and had fondue for dinner.
